How to play

Host.

An idle game about being the worst thing that ever happened to a planet.

You are a virus. Get into one host, drain its lifeforce, and spread to the next. Spend what you drain on upgrades: more damage, longer jumps, stranger mutations.

Each world fights back with an immune system and a race to a cure. Lose all your viruses and the run ends, but the DNA you earned stays and makes every future run stronger.

Clear five zones, from a petri dish to the whole planet. A boss guards the end of each one. Kill Patient Omega and the world is yours.

You can play every choice yourself, or leave it running. Anything you don't decide gets decided for you, and it keeps playing while you're away.
